# Hindi puts "end" after the period with a postposition, and the period goes
# into the oblique case, which CLDR has only where its own word already is
# ("इस माह"): the aliases put the others back ("अगले माह का अंत"). A time takes
# no word. An ordinal weekday is not read.
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges, the day before or after a point, the weekend (Sunday alone in India)
# and an ordinal weekday with the month first read. Hindi puts the point before a
# duration, which after and before do not take.
