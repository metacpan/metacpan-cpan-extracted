# Time::Keeper::Natural word table: ar
#
# UNREVIEWED: written 18 Oct 2026 by a session, as the p2p site's catalogues were.
# A native reader should review it before release. The format is en.txt's.
#
# Arabic joins "end" to a period by idafa with no word between, and CLDR's
# words carry their article, so "نهاية الشهر الماضي" reads as it stands. An
# ordinal weekday is not read.
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges, a duration before or after a point, the weekend (CLDR's week words with
# "عطلة نهاية الأسبوع") and an ordinal weekday after its weekday ("الجمعة الثالثة
# من الشهر القادم") read. A duration is read in CLDR's forms ("3 أسابيع"), so the
# oblique dual "أسبوعين" does not.
start_of	بداية|في بداية|مطلع|في مطلع
end_of	نهاية|في نهاية
at	الساعة|في الساعة
on	يوم|في يوم
skip
and
range	من {0} إلى {1}|من {0} حتى {1}|بين {0} و{1}|{0} حتى {1}
after	{0} بعد {1}
before	{0} قبل {1}
weekend	عطلة نهاية الأسبوع
nth	{weekday} {n} من {period}|{weekday} {n} في {period}
last	الأخيرة|الأخير
