# French contracts "de le" to "du": "fin du mois dernier" is read by putting
# the swallowed "le" back, since CLDR's own words carry it ("le mois dernier").
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges, a duration before or after a point, "le lendemain" and "la veille"
# read. The weekend does not: "week-end" is masculine and CLDR's "la semaine
# prochaine" feminine.

end_of	fin de|fin du|fin des|fin d'|la fin de|la fin du|la fin des|la fin d'|à la fin de|à la fin du|à la fin des|à la fin d'
at	à
on
skip	le|la|les|l'
and
of	de|du|d'

after	{0} après {1}|le lendemain de {0}
before	{0} avant {1}|la veille de {0}
