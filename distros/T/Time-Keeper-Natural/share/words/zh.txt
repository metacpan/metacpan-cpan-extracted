# Chinese puts "end" and "beginning" after the period. "周末" is the weekend,
# and reads as the end of the week. A time and a day take no word. An ordinal
# weekday ("下个月的第三个星期五") is not read.
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges, the day before or after a point and an ordinal weekday read. Chinese
# puts the point before a duration, which after and before do not take. "下周末"
# stays the end of next week, as 0.01 read it, so there is no weekend line.
start_of	{0}初
end_of	{0}底|{0}末
at
on
skip
and
range	从{0}到{1}|{0}到{1}|{0}至{1}
after	{0}的后一天
before	{0}的前一天
nth	{period}的第{n}个{weekday}|{period}第{n}个{weekday}|{period}的{n}个{weekday}
last	最后一
