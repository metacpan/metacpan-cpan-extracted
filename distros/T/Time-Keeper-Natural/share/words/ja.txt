#
# UNREVIEWED: written 18 Oct 2026 by a session, as the p2p site's catalogues were.
# A native reader should review it before release. The format is en.txt's.
#
# Japanese puts "end" and "beginning" after the period. "週末" is the weekend,
# and reads as the end of the week. An ordinal weekday puts the month first
# ("来月の第3金曜日"), the nth frame's order.
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges and the day before or after a point read. Japanese puts the point
# before a duration, which after and before do not take. "来週末" stays the end
# of next week, as 0.01 read it, so there is no weekend line.
# "の" joins a day to a time ("明日の15時"), at its first "の" only, so "来週の火曜日
# の15時" does not read.
start_of	{0}初め|{0}の初め|{0}始め|{0}の始め|{0}初旬
end_of	{0}末|{0}の末|{0}の終わり
at	{0}に|の
on	{0}に
skip
and
last	最後の|最終
nth	{period}の第{n}{weekday}|{period}の{n}{weekday}
range	{0}から{1}まで|{0}から{1}|{0}～{1}
after	{0}の翌日
before	{0}の前日
