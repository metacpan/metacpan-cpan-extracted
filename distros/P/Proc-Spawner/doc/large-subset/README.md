# Proc::ShellSubset::Large: the large parser, its specification and its measurements

Written with Claude by the parser session (file prefix `large-subset-`), 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-02. The module was
called `ShellSubsetLarge` until the distribution moved every module under `Proc::` (2026-11-02). The current
documents below use the new names; the historical ones keep the names of their day.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| [`large-subset-spec.md`](large-subset-spec.md) | **The specification** (current): what the large grammar accepts beyond `Proc::ShellSubset`, the rules where dash and bash differ (A3), the tree additions, the rules for the executor at run time (A4: status 248, A5: as dash), tests and measurements. |
| [`large-subset-state.md`](large-subset-state.md) | **The state of the union** (current): the rules and which beats which, the yardsticks decided by the shells alone, the figures, the work list, subtle and open verdicts, blatant differences; section 7: **the behaviour**, every accepted sample run in dash, bash and ours (the table of post-0.09; the same for the in-process programs of 0.10, IP1; W5, the event loop, checked over it on Linux and what ran under Wine; ours through Run; the atomic calls). |
| [`large-subset-logical.md`](large-subset-logical.md) | **Rule E** (current): where dash and bash differ, the plainly logical behaviour, by category, item by item; the earlier decisions checked against it. |
| [`large-subset-environment.md`](large-subset-environment.md) | **The samples and the run's environment** (known issue X27, current): for every accepted corpus sample, whether the run-time comparison could come out otherwise in another environment (strict, almost strict, undecided), the codes, how to remake it; section 9: the six kinds of workaround and the runs with the environment varied (other cases of `POSIXLY_CORRECT`, the locale, SIGPIPE, `/proc`; fresh or private places; stand-ins for programs; fixed file times), footnotes to the totals of section 1 (EV1). |
| [`large-subset-provenance.tsv`](large-subset-provenance.tsv) | Its record: one line per accepted corpus sample, by snippet ID, with a short code; the header says how to read it. |
| [`tools/large-subset-env-variants.tsv`](tools/large-subset-env-variants.tsv) | Every sample whose result changed when the run's environment was varied (section 9 there), by variant. |
| [`tools/large-subset-run-differences.tsv`](tools/large-subset-run-differences.tsv) | The samples of the behaviour run where ours differs from both shells, or the shells differ and ours agrees with neither, or the messages are worded as neither's. |
| [`large-subset-dialect-features.md`](large-subset-dialect-features.md) | **dash vs bash, feature by feature** (current): every probed feature, blatant or subtle, with who decided it; the per-test record. |
| [`tools/`](tools) | The measurement scripts (not run by `make test`): [`large-subset-extract.pl`](tools/large-subset-extract.pl) (corpora to `samples.jsonl`), [`large-subset-scan.pl`](tools/large-subset-scan.pl) (both shells, ShellCheck, both parsers; `--rx STRING` tries the regex detector), [`large-subset-drops.pl`](tools/large-subset-drops.pl) (left-out samples with reasons), [`large-subset-remeasure.pl`](tools/large-subset-remeasure.pl), [`large-subset-yardsticks.pl`](tools/large-subset-yardsticks.pl) (the yardsticks, decided by the shells), [`large-subset-features.pl`](tools/large-subset-features.pl) (the detector of dash/bash differences, and the per-test record), [`large-subset-verdicts.txt`](tools/large-subset-verdicts.txt) (verdicts kept by hand, also postponed free-form ones), [`large-subset-noise.txt`](tools/large-subset-noise.txt) (samples left out by hand as noise: placeholders, `a|b|c` alternatives, cut-off here-documents; post2-0.04), [`large-subset-reparse.pl`](tools/large-subset-reparse.pl) (refresh the parser fields after a parser change), the run-time comparison ([`large-subset-runcompare.pl`](tools/large-subset-runcompare.pl): `-env` varies the environment, `-run` goes through Run, `-shells` and `-workers-from` run ours alone after a change of ours; [`large-subset-runreport.pl`](tools/large-subset-runreport.pl): `-b` against a base run, `-ours-from` joins ours of such a run with an earlier run's dash and bash, `-ours-vs` compares ours of two runs; [`large-subset-threeway.pl`](tools/large-subset-threeway.pl), [`large-subset-builtins.pl`](tools/large-subset-builtins.pl)) and [`large-subset-blocks.pl`](tools/large-subset-blocks.pl) (the levels of [`large-subset-environment.md`](large-subset-environment.md)). How to run them: [`../test-pools.md`](../test-pools.md). |
| [`dialect-cases/`](dialect-cases) | The probes: the examples of the page "Shell Dialect Choices" (in Proc-Spawner 0.03) ([`cases.txt`](dialect-cases/cases.txt), [`results.txt`](dialect-cases/results.txt)) and 86 more ([`large-subset-dialect-probes2.txt`](dialect-cases/large-subset-dialect-probes2.txt), `-results2.txt`), with their output in dash 0.5.12 and `bash --posix` 5.2.21 ([`run-cases.sh`](dialect-cases/run-cases.sh) reruns them). |
| (removed) | `pages/`, the sources of the checklist page "Shell Corpus Gaps" (in Proc-Spawner 0.04; removed after it with the page).
