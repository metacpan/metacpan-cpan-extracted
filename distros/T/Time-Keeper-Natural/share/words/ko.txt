# Time::Keeper::Natural word table: ko
#
# UNREVIEWED: written 18 Oct 2026 by a session, as the p2p site's catalogues were.
# A native reader should review it before release. The format is en.txt's.
#
# Korean puts "end" and "beginning" after the period, and "에" after a time or
# a day. An ordinal weekday is not read.
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges, the day before or after a point and an ordinal weekday with the
# month first read. Korean puts the point before a duration, which after and
# before do not take. "다음 주말" stays the end of next week, as 0.01 read it, so
# there is no weekend line.
start_of	{0} 초|{0}초
end_of	{0} 말|{0}말
at	{0}에
on	{0}에
skip
and
range	{0}부터 {1}까지|{0}에서 {1}까지
after	{0} 다음 날
before	{0} 전날
nth	{period} {n} {weekday}
last	마지막
