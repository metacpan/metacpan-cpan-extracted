=head1 NAME

App::FuguSeed - make BIP39 seed words with dice, and turn them into a SeedQR

=head1 SYNOPSIS

    fuguseed-last
    fuguseed-qr

=head1 DESCRIPTION

FuguSeed makes BIP39 seed words with three dice, on paper, and turns
them into a Standard SeedQR. C<fuguseed-last> reads words 1 to 11 and
the YELLOW and BLUE faces of word 12 on standard input, and it prints
the check word. C<fuguseed-qr> reads the 12 words on standard input
and prints the SeedQR as text, zone by zone, so a person can draw it
on paper. These two programs see the words, so they run on an
air-gapped computer only. See L<fuguseed-last(1)> and
L<fuguseed-qr(1)> for the input, the result, and the exit codes.

This is the lead module of the App-FuguSeed distribution. It holds no
code of its own. The work is split by concern:

=over 4

=item L<App::FuguSeed::List>

The 2048 words of the English BIP39 list, and their digest.

=item L<App::FuguSeed::Mnemonic>

The word checks, the indexes, and the checksum.

=item L<App::FuguSeed::Last>

The face check, the check word, and the flow of C<fuguseed-last>.

=item L<App::FuguSeed::Codewords>

The data codewords and the error correction codewords.

=item L<App::FuguSeed::Matrix>

The module grid of the code.

=item L<App::FuguSeed::Text>

The grid view and the zone views.

=item L<App::FuguSeed::QR>

The checksum check, the digit string, and the flow of C<fuguseed-qr>.

=back

The release ships C<fuguseed-last> and C<fuguseed-qr> as two packed
files that C<scripts/pack> builds from these modules. Each file needs
no install step and no module outside the base of perl.
