# Bengali joins the genitive to the period ("মাসের") and "end" after it, so the
# frames carry the suffix. A time takes no word. An ordinal weekday is not read.
#
# 0.02's keys: Ranges and the weekend read. Bengali puts the point before a duration, which
# after and before do not take, and CLDR has no Bengali ordinal words, so an
# ordinal weekday is not read.
start_of	{0}ের শুরু|{0}ের শুরুতে|{0}-এর শুরু
end_of	{0}ের শেষ|{0}ের শেষে|{0}-এর শেষ
at
on	{0}ে|{0}-এ
skip
and
range	{0} থেকে {1} পর্যন্ত|{0} থেকে {1}
weekend	সপ্তাহান্ত
